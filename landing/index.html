Integration

Add AI in Minutes, Not Months

Three ways to connect — pick what works for you.

Just add a link to your hosted SevakAI portal. Your customers click it and submit their messages.

<!-- Add this anywhere on your website --> <a href="https://sevak.ai/your-business">Contact Support</a>

Drop one script tag and a floating "Contact Us" button appears on your site. Zero configuration.

<!-- Paste before </body> tag --> <script src="https://cdn.sevak.ai/widget.js" data-id="YOUR_WORKSPACE_ID"> </script>

Full REST API for developers. Post a message, get back classification, urgency, and ticket ID.

fetch('https://api.sevak.ai/v1/classify', { method: 'POST', headers: { 'Authorization': 'Bearer API_KEY' }, body: JSON.stringify({ text: "Where is my order?", workspace: "your-business" }) });
